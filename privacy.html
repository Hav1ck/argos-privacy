Privacy policy

Argos is a personal app used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

Google requires a public home page and privacy policy for apps that use its sign-in, which is the only reason these pages are published here.

It reads the owner's own calendar and email so it can show them to the owner. It does not share this data with anyone, does not sell it and does not use it for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under third-party connections.
